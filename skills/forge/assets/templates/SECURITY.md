## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities.

- GitHub: use [Report a Vulnerability](https://github.com/{{OWNER}}/{{REPO}}/security/advisories/new) (requires private vulnerability reporting to be enabled under Settings -> Code security).
- GitLab: open a confidential issue or email {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

What to include: affected version or commit, reproduction steps, and impact as you understand it.

What to expect:

- acknowledgement within {{ACK_SLA_DAYS}} business days
- a triage decision and remediation plan for confirmed reports
- credit in release notes unless you prefer otherwise

Please give us reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure.
